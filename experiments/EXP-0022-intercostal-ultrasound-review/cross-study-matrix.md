# Cross-study matrix

| Source | Population/design | Site and mode | Respiratory contrast | Main finding | Applicability boundary |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cala 1998 | 4 healthy; physiology | 2nd bilateral parasternal; B-mode | quiet inspiration | ventral motion/straightening without thickness change | mechanics only; very small sample |
| Wallbridge 2018 | 20 stable COPD; cross-sectional | 2nd/3rd bilateral parasternal; B-mode | fixed end-tidal inspiration | reproducible thickness; modest FEV1 associations | experienced operators; no control/outcome validation |
| Yoshida 2019 | 12 healthy men; paired | anterior/lateral/posterior right; B-mode | rest vs maximal inspiration | thickening only at anterior sites | maximal effort; layers unresolved laterally/posteriorly |
| Dres 2020 | 23 healthy + 16/54 ventilated; physiology/diagnostic | 2nd right parasternal; M-mode | support levels, diaphragm function, SBT | TFic tracks unloading and compensatory recruitment | SBT association; no treatment-effect evidence |
| Valera-Calero 2021 | 59 healthy; prediction | posterior rhomboid point; B-mode | maximal inspiration vs expiration | pleura is shallower on inspiration | one procedural site; no external validation |
| Ramaswamy 2023 | retracted | parasternal | not eligible | attachment is a 2025 retraction notice | no result may support synthesis |
| Huang 2024 | 59 liver patients; repeated observation | 7th/8th anterior/mid-axillary; B-mode/SWE | end inspiration vs expiration | inspiration widens and stiffens ICS; SCD effect is site-dependent | liver TE window, not muscle effort |
| Hoermann 2026 | 38 healthy; randomized phases | parasternal + diaphragm; B-mode | three loads and five NIV settings | ITF does not track delta-Poes or load | young healthy; not diseased NIV population |

## Comparable patterns

- Cala, Yoshida and Hoermann jointly show that mechanical activation does not
  imply a context-independent thickness response: quiet/moderate tasks and
  maximal inspiration are not interchangeable.
- Dres and Hoermann differ by diaphragm state and population rather than forming
  a direct contradiction: TFic is informative with compensatory recruitment but
  not in healthy subjects with preserved diaphragm function.
- Valera-Calero and Huang independently show respiratory and site dependence of
  an intercostal acoustic/procedural window, but measure different anatomical
  endpoints and cannot be numerically pooled.
- Wallbridge supports repeatability at fixed parasternal sites, while Yoshida,
  Valera-Calero and Huang warn against transferring values across sites.
