Publication 001: Retrieval practice in a short online module

Methods: A small randomized classroom study compared retrieval-practice prompts
with rereading after a two-week module. The source reports the assignment and
the assessment window in its Methods section.

Results: The retrieval-practice group scored higher on the delayed quiz than
the rereading group. The paper reports the group comparison in Results.

Discussion: The authors interpret the result as support for retrieval prompts
in this narrow module, while noting that the sample and follow-up are limited.
