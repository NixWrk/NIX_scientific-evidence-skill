Publication 002: Worked examples for introductory laboratory reasoning

Methods: A controlled teaching study compared worked examples with unguided
problem solving during an introductory laboratory unit. Outcomes were measured
with a post-unit reasoning task.

Results: Students receiving worked examples completed the reasoning task with
fewer procedural errors. The publication reports the comparison in its Results.

Discussion: The authors conclude that worked examples can support novice
laboratory reasoning in this setting, while cautioning that transfer beyond the
unit was not established.
