# Genre: decision log

`mode: record` · `genre: decision-log` · no organizational records

## Purpose

Record why a choice was made, at the moment it was made. The reader is the
author six months later, or a reviewer asking why a different method was not
used. An entry that records only the outcome is worthless for both.

## Input

Require the decision, the alternatives that were actually considered, and
whatever grounded the choice: supplied constraints, literature, or own results.

## Procedure

1. State the decision as one sentence in the past tense.
2. Record the date, because a decision is only defensible against what was
   known then.
3. List the alternatives that were actually weighed. An alternative invented
   afterwards to make the choice look considered is not recorded.
4. Record the ground of the choice with its reference: which constraint, which
   source, which result.
5. Record what the choice costs: what it makes harder or forecloses.
6. Record reversibility: whether the decision can be undone, and at what point
   it becomes expensive to undo.
7. Record what would change the decision.

## The ground is a reference, not a feeling

"Chosen for convenience" and "seemed more suitable" record nothing. Where the
ground is a constraint, cite it. Where it is literature, cite the source. Where
it is a preference with no evidence behind it, record that plainly — an honest
"no evidence, chosen by preference" is a usable entry, an unsupported claim of
superiority is not.

## Reversibility is part of the record

A decision that can be undone in an hour and a decision that determines the
shape of the whole work are different objects. Recording which one this is
tells a later reader how hard to argue with it.

## Do not rewrite history

An entry is written once and superseded, never edited to match what turned out
to be true. When the decision is reversed, write a new entry that refers to the
old one, keeping both. A log that only contains decisions that turned out well
is not a log.

## Gates

- At least one source: the ground of the choice is recorded somewhere.
- No organizational records: this genre is about method, not about conferences,
  publications, or registrations.
- Internal references resolve to existing units.
