# Scientific judgment calibration

Load this reference only for evidence-dependent findings involving inference,
sample roles, repeated observations, nested objects or severity.

## Sample roles

Distinguish development/training, threshold selection, internal checking,
external validation and repeated measurements. Thresholds selected on healthy
participants and applied to patients are distinct roles, but not automatically
an independent external validation.

## Unit of analysis

Distinguish participant, recording, session, cycle and measurement. Many cycles
are not many independent participants. Look for aggregation or a repeated-
measures/hierarchical model. If this is not reported, criticise the reporting
gap; reserve “pseudoreplication” for a demonstrated mismatch.

## Hierarchy and scope

Record the level of each compared object. A parent basin and its branch are
related but not identical resolutions. Prefer “cross-level comparison; exact
localization is not established” to an unsupported claim of incomparability.

## Absence

Separate “not observed in the checked scope”, “not found after a complete
search” and “not assessed”. Add “normatively required but absent” only when an
applicable versioned requirement and complete search both exist.

## Severity

- `critical`: the key result becomes numerically or logically wrong;
- `major`: evidence for a main conclusion is materially weakened;
- `minor`: a local defect does not change the main conclusion;
- `note`: limitation, observation or recommendation.

Confidence in a fact and severity of its consequence are different. “Not
reported” does not mean “did not happen”.
