# Dissertation release gates

## Scientific gates

- Every material claim resolves to evidence or an approved result.
- Numbers retain value, unit, denominator, group, time point and locator.
- Methods come from protocol/data records; Results come from approved results.
- Tasks map to results and conclusions; novelty and propositions remain bounded.
- Terminology, causal strength, limitations and conflicts remain consistent.

## Critic gates

- Run each critic against the rules of the skill that owns the artifact.
- Resolve or explicitly accept every critical/major finding.
- Technical findings retain their deterministic audit when one exists.
- Editorial findings retain a locator and rationale.
- Evidence-dependent findings retain source, calculation, coverage or authority
  verification; ordinary editorial findings do not require it.

## Normative and apparatus gates

- Applicable authorities remain separate from observed defended practice.
- Title page, contents, abbreviations, terminology, bibliography, illustrations
  and appendices close against the approved manuscript and selected profile.
- Page numbers are checked only after layout in a paginated output.

## Delivery gates

The report format is selected independently of the criticism. Word comments or
tracked changes are optional. When DOCX is selected, preserve the original,
verify exact anchors and issue IDs, audit OOXML, open-save in a compatible
engine and inspect rendered pages. Other formats do not inherit Word-specific
requirements.

No required stage may remain pending/blocked and no applicable gate may remain
`not_assessed` at release.
