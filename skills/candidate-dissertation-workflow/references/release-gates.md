# Dissertation release gates

## Scientific gates

- Every material claim resolves to verified literature evidence or an approved
  research result.
- Every number retains value, unit, denominator, group, time point and source
  locator where applicable.
- Methods are supported by protocol/data records; Results are supported by
  result records; interpretation is not presented as observation.
- Every declared task maps to at least one result and one conclusion.
- Novelty is bounded by the actual search corpus and anchored to own results.
- Defense propositions are falsifiable statements with result and structure
  anchors, not topics or chapter summaries.
- The conclusion and synopsis introduce no claim absent from the dissertation.
- Terminology, causal strength, limitations and conflicts remain consistent.

## Normative and apparatus gates

- Applicable authorities are versioned and separated from observed defended
  practice.
- Title-page fields are checked against approved facts and selected local
  authorities.
- The final TOC derives from Word heading styles and is refreshed after layout;
  page-number currency is visually checked.
- Abbreviation, terminology, citation/bibliography, illustration/table and
  appendix registers close against the final manuscript.
- Conditional lists are included only when the corresponding objects exist.

## Word and release gates

- The original DOCX and source hashes are preserved.
- Critic findings pass the PSES validator; every Word annotation has an exact
  anchor and one issue ID.
- Comments remain comments; tracked changes are used only for exact local
  replacements and are not accepted automatically.
- The final DOCX opens and saves in Word or a compatible layout engine.
- Every rendered page is inspected; OOXML comments/revisions are audited
  structurally because rendering alone cannot prove their presence.
- No required stage is pending or blocked and no gate remains `not_assessed` at
  release. A consciously inapplicable stage is recorded as `not_applicable`,
  never silently omitted.
